I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Virginia, where I am a graduate research assistant in the [LESS Lab](https://less-lab-uva.github.io/), advised by [Professor Matthew B. Dwyer](https://engineering.virginia.edu/faculty/matthew-b-dwyer). The lab's research spans verification and validation of machine-learned models, robotic systems, and software more broadly.

My own research broadly focuses on **autoformalization, formal methods, testing, and verification of software-intensive systems**. I am particularly interested in automatically translating natural-language requirements into formal specifications, such as temporal logic, and using those specifications to systematically test, verify, and analyze system behavior. I have applied these ideas in domains such as **autonomous vehicles**, while my broader goal is to develop general methods for making **software testing and verification more formally grounded, systematic, rigorous, and scalable**.

For additional information, please see my [CV]({{ base_path }}/files/cv.pdf).
