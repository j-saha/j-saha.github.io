Clustering cells into homogeneous subpopulations from single-cell RNA-seq data is a core step in analyzing cellular heterogeneity, but existing clustering methods perform inconsistently across datasets and are rarely probabilistic, making it hard to quantify uncertainty in the resulting clusters. We propose a generative, probabilistic clustering approach for scRNA-seq data that handles dropout events directly and does not require prior marker-gene information.
